Specialized Metrics for Digital Forensics (Conceptual)

Forensic metrics focus on subtle, often imperceptible artifacts to reveal an image's origin and processing history, ignoring semantic content.

  • Photo-Response Non-Uniformity (PRNU): A unique noise pattern from microscopic imperfections in a camera's sensor, acting as a device-level fingerprint. Extremely subtle and requires advanced denoising and cross-correlation techniques for extraction.
  • Color Filter Array (CFA) Interpolation: Traces left by proprietary demosaicing algorithms used by camera ISPs to reconstruct full color from single-color sensor pixels.
  • Lens Distortion: Geometric aberrations unique to a lens model.
  • JPEG Quantization Tables: Specific compression tables used by camera's ISP, extractable from JPEG headers.
  • Image Forgery Detection (e.g., Copy-Move, ELA): Techniques to detect tampering. Error Level Analysis (ELA) looks for inconsistencies in JPEG compression levels.

Implementation Note: These techniques are highly specialized and often computationally intensive, requiring deep image processing knowledge and specific algorithms (e.g., advanced denoising for PRNU, complex parsing for JPEG headers, block matching for copy-move). They are generally beyond the scope of a simple demonstration tool.
